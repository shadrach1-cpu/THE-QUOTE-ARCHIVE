The curated cards are the archive's filed collection. The generator does not pretend to store millions of historical quotes; it weaves new combinations on demand from its phrase banks.

Human Quote Library: local archive ready — expanded human-made quotations can be imported and retained locally.

The expanded human library is designed as a local-first, multi-source collection with a target capacity of up to 5,000,000 imported records. Imported records are deduplicated, quality-filtered and stored in the browser's IndexedDB vault when supported. Source and license metadata are retained for provenance; QuoteKG research access may be used as an external acquisition source when available; no single external service is required for normal archive operation.
