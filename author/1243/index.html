Epictetus

10 quotes in The Quote Archive. Search in the archive

  • “Common and vulgar people ascribe all ill that they feel to others; people of little wisdom ascribe to themselves; people of much wisdom, to no one.”
  • “The materials of action are variable, but the use we make of them should be constant.”
  • “Whenever you are angry, be assured that it is not only a present evil, but that you have increased a habit.”
  • “A ship should not ride on a single anchor, nor life on a single hope.”
  • “Every habit and faculty is preserved and increased by correspondent actions,—as the habit of walking, by walking; of running, by running.”
  • “The appearance of things to the mind is the standard of every action to man.”
  • “Choose the life that is noblest, for custom can make it sweet to thee.”
  • “If you would be a good reader, read; if a writer, write.”
  • “Whatever you would make habitual, practice it; and if you would not make a thing habitual, do not practice it, but accustom yourself to something else.”
  • “Practice yourself, for heaven's sake, in little things; and thence proceed to greater.”
